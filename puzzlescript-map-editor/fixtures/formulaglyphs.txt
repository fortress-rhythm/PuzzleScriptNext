(A deliberately hostile legend. Every one of these characters is legal in a
PuzzleScript level and every one of them means something to a spreadsheet:
'=' '+' '-' and '@' all start a formula in Excel, ',' and '"' break naive CSV
splitting, and '\' is an escape in plenty of formats. If a round trip survives
this file it will survive a real game.)
