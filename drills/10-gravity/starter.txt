  CONCEPT  again  at the end of a rule asks for another turn straight after
           this one, with no key pressed: each extra turn is one frame of
           animation. Extra turns keep coming while some rule with again
           matches and changes something.
           stationary X  matches an X that is not moving this turn.

  TASK     A boulder with nothing under it falls until it lands on a wall,
           another boulder, or the player. The player can push boulders
           sideways (that rule is written for you).

  HINT     Step 1: a late rule that moves a boulder down into an empty cell.
           That passes the check, but the boulder drops all the way in one
           frame, because a rule repeats until it stops matching.
           Step 2: make it fall one cell per frame, by giving it a movement
           and asking for again. Now a boulder you push off a ledge hangs
           in the air until your next key press. Work out why (it was
           moving when your rule looked at it) and give it the extra frame
           it needs.
