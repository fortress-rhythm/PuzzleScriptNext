title Drill 03: Chain push
author drills

(
  CONCEPT  A property in the LEGEND names a group of objects:
               Pushable = Crate or Barrel
           A rule that mentions Pushable matches either one, and the same
           Pushable on the right-hand side means "whichever one it was".
           Rules repeat until they stop matching, so one rule can ripple
           down a whole row.

  TASK     The player pushes crates and barrels. Anything being pushed also
           pushes the crate or barrel in front of it, so a whole row moves.
           If the front of the row is against a wall, nothing moves.

  HINT     Add the Pushable property to the LEGEND, then two rules: one for
           the player pushing, one for a pushed thing pushing.

  CHECK    node drills/check.js 03
)
