title Drill 07: Keys and doors
author drills

(
  CONCEPT  State lives in objects. "The player is carrying a key" is a
           HasKey object sitting in the player's cell.
           A rule can have several separate parts, matched anywhere:
               [ A ] [ B ] -> [ ] [ B A ]     moves A to wherever B is

  TASK     Walk over a key k to pick it up: the key vanishes and the player
           gets a HasKey. HasKey must stay with the player wherever they go,
           including when they bump a wall. Walking into a door D while
           carrying a key opens it: the door and the HasKey both vanish and
           the player steps in. Without a key, doors are walls. You can only
           carry one key; walk over a second one and it stays on the floor.

  HINT     Three rules. Watch out: HasKey is on its own layer, so if you
           give it a movement it can walk into a wall the player bumped.

  CHECK    node drills/check.js 07
)
