title Drill 05: Holes
author drills

(
  CONCEPT  Each turn runs in order: your rules, then movement, then any
           rules marked  late . A normal rule sees the board before anything
           has moved; a late rule sees where things ended up.

  TASK     A crate pushed into a hole fills it: the crate and the hole both
           vanish on that same turn, leaving floor. The player cannot walk
           into an open hole.

  HINT     The push rule is written for you. Add one rule that blocks the
           player, and one that fills the hole. Try the second without
           late first and watch what happens.

  CHECK    node drills/check.js 05
)
