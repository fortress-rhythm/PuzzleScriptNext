title Drill 01: Push
author drills

(
  CONCEPT  A rule is a pattern and a replacement:  [ match ] -> [ result ]
           Cells next to each other are separated by  |
           >  means "moving the way the rule points". A rule with no
           direction word is tried facing all four ways.

  TASK     Make the player push crates. Walking into a crate moves it one
           cell the same way. A crate against a wall, or against another
           crate, stays put, and so does the player.

  HINT     One rule. Say what happens to the crate: the engine already stops
           anything that moves into something solid on its layer.

  CHECK    node drills/check.js 01
)
